What the law actually allows you to ask

Khala is a service dog under the ADA, not a pet or an emotional support animal. When it's not obvious what she does, staff may ask only two questions:

  • 01 Is the dog a service animal required because of a disability?
  • 02 What work or task has the dog been trained to perform?

Staff may not ask about the handler's disability, require a demonstration of the task, or ask for documentation as a condition of entry.

NOTE No certification, registration, ID, or vest is legally required for a service dog in the U.S. — and no government registry for one exists. This page is a courtesy from her handler, not proof of anything the law requires.
